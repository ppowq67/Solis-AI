Refund Policy

Effective July 2026

This Refund Policy explains how Solis AI subscriptions work, how cancellation affects billing, and when refunds may apply. Paid plans are processed by Paddle (our Merchant of Record). This page should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1. Billing through Paddle

Subscriptions and one-time charges for Solis AI are handled by Paddle. Paddle appears on your statement as the seller for tax and payment purposes. Card details and tax invoices are managed through Paddle’s checkout and customer portal, not stored by Solis as your card processor.

2. Subscriptions and cancellation

  • Cancel anytime. You can cancel a paid subscription at any time from your Solis account billing settings or through the Paddle customer portal linked from your receipt.
  • Cancellation stops the next renewal. When you cancel, you keep access to your plan benefits through the end of the current paid billing period. You will not be charged for the next renewal.
  • Canceling does not by itself trigger a refund for the period already paid. See section 3.

3. Refunds

Generally, we do not offer refunds after a billing period has started, except where required by law (for example certain consumer cooling-off rights in your country) or where Solis expressly offers a refund in writing.

  • If you cancel mid-cycle, the current period usually remains non-refundable, and access continues until that period ends.
  • If a charge was made in error, was unauthorized, or you believe you are entitled to a refund under applicable law, contact us promptly at help@solisai.video with your Paddle receipt or order ID.
  • Where a refund is approved, it is typically processed through Paddle to the original payment method. Timing depends on Paddle and your bank or card issuer.

4. Free plan and trials

Free-plan usage is not billed. If we offer a free trial on a paid plan, cancel before the trial ends (using the same cancel flow) to avoid the first paid renewal, unless the trial terms say otherwise.

5. Contact

Billing or refund questions: help@solisai.video. Include the email on your Solis account and your Paddle order or receipt ID when possible.
