Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about turning long videos into ready-to-post shorts with Solis AI.

What makes Solis AI different from other clip tools?

Solis AI is an agentic content-repurposing AI. It understands your video, chooses moments, removes distractions, and produces platform-ready shorts — with post-generation editing so you stay in control.

What video platforms are supported?

YouTube and YouTube Shorts today. TikTok and Instagram are on the roadmap.

Can I edit after generation?

Yes. Customize text, captions, layout, and more without re-running the full generation.
